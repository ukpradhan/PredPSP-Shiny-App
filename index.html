Photosynthetic proteins (PSP) in plants are the architects of life on Earth. They capture the boundless energy of the sun, turning it into the chemical currency of life. The interconnected and highly specialized network of proteins involved in photosynthesis ensures the efficient utilization of this energy. This intricate dance of light and proteins not only powers plant growth but also provides oxygen and serves as a foundation for all life forms. Understanding the roles and structures of these photosynthetic proteins continues to be a vibrant area of research, offering insights into both fundamental biology and potential applications in renewable energy and agriculture. Thus, the PredPSP is developed for predicting the Photosynthetic proteins in C3 and C4 plant. The the light-gradient boosting method (LGBM) has been adopted for prediction purpose, where combination of suitable sequence derived feature was used to represent each PSP sequence as a numeric feature vectors.

Citation: Meher, P.K., Pradhan, U.K., Sethi, P.L. et al. PredPSP: a novel computational tool to discover pathway-specific photosynthetic proteins in plants. Plant Mol Biol 114, 106 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11103-024-01500-6
